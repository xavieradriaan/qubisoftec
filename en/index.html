ERP · Business management Live in production

Orion ERP

The operational backbone of the whole company

Who it's for
Companies with fieldwork, warehouses, and projects
What it solves
Departments working with disconnected information
Scope
6 modules · 8 access profiles

Connects sales, projects, purchasing, and inventory in a single flow. An accepted quote becomes a work order; the work order generates requisitions; requisitions move real inventory from the warehouses; and every cost is reflected in the project's budget. Full traceability is kept at every stage.

  • Sales & CRM. Real-time quotes, sent by email with a branded document, and discount approval with the responsible person's signature.
  • Projects & work orders. Budget by category, actual cost vs. planned, milestones, labor hours, and closeout with a digital signature.
  • Purchasing. Requests, comparison of supplier offers, automatic purchase order generation, and partial or full receiving.
  • Multi-warehouse inventory. Available, reserved, and in-transit inventory, transfers, returns, and tool loans with on-screen signature.
  • Permissions & traceability. Eight roles with per-module permissions, a single session per user, and a change history on every record.
  • Reports. PDF and Excel exports with issuer and recipient signatures, ready to file or audit.
Human resources · Attendance tracking Live in production

Orion Biometría

Attendance tracking without equipment investment

Who it's for
Teams with field staff or multiple locations
What it solves
Expensive biometric clocks and manual logs
Deployment
No hardware or on-site installation

Employees clock in and out from their own phone: a photo verifies their identity and location confirms where they are. No biometric clocks, no fingerprint readers, and no on-site installation. No photos are stored either — only a mathematical code derived from the face is kept, one that cannot be used to reconstruct the image.

  • Individual facial verification. Confirms each employee's identity with high accuracy, validated through testing.
  • Per-employee geofence. Each person has an assigned check-in point and allowed radius; the system logs the exact distance.
  • Automatic punctuality tracking. Configurable grace period for lateness, early departures, and overtime calculated automatically.
  • Monthly reports. Days worked, minutes late, and overtime per employee, exportable to PDF and Excel with your company's branding.
  • Privacy by design. Photo evidence is optional per company, and a worker's face data can be deleted on request.
  • Minimal operating cost. Runs on lightweight infrastructure, with support for multiple companies and time zones.

Personal finance Live in production

Contabilízate

Personal finance tracking with real credit-card cycles

Who it's for
People managing several credit cards
What it solves
Not knowing the real amount due each month
Scope
Income, expenses, cards, and installment plans

Most expense-tracking apps just add and subtract. This one reproduces how a credit card actually works: it distinguishes what has already been billed from what hasn't yet, so the figure it shows matches the actual payment due for the period.

  • Real credit-card cycles: billing and due dates, separating what's already billed from what isn't yet.
  • Interest-free installment plans, with grace months and automatic monthly accrual.
  • Encrypted credential vault, protected with additional email verification.

Quotes with corporate identity, generated instantly

Who it's for

What it solves

Status

You fill in the details, add the line items, and get a professionally formatted document: aligned amounts, accounting-grade typography, and automatic pagination. Totals are calculated and verified on the server.

Featured case

CITELL S.A. · Telecommunications & infrastructure, Guayaquil

Operated with spreadsheets split by department: sales didn't know warehouse availability, purchasing received information late, and the real cost of each job was known only after it was too late to correct it.

The challenge

Each department kept its own file and its own version of the information. Material requisitions traveled by email, approvals by messaging apps, and a job's budget was closed weeks after it was finished.

What we built

A custom ERP with sales, projects, purchasing, inventory, approvals, and document management on a single source of truth, with role-based permissions and approvals handled inside the system instead of by email.

The result

  • 6 modules chained into a single workflow
  • 3 warehouses synced in real time
  • 8 roles with granular permissions
  • 121 automatic notices that used to be manual

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us before getting started

Can I try the system before hiring it?

Depending on the project, we can run a demo or present a functional prototype so you can see how the system would work before making a decision.

Can I start with just one module and grow later?

Yes. We design the system to be modular, so you can start with the features you need most and add new modules as your operation grows.

Do you integrate with SRI, Civil Registry, IESS, ANT, SERCOP, Companies Registry, etc.?

Yes. We connect your systems to these entities' web services to validate and look up data in real time, without manual entry or transcription errors. Each integration is scoped according to the project.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, but we work module by module: the first one is typically live within six to eight weeks, and we deliver every two weeks from then on. A full ERP like Orion is built over several months, and the client uses it starting from the first module delivered.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes, and it's common — a system in use always generates new requirements. We offer hourly support or a monthly agreement, depending on how fast your operation changes.

Where is the system hosted?

Wherever you prefer. We've deployed on clients' own servers, on AWS, and on low-cost infrastructure. If you don't have infrastructure of your own, we propose the best option and set it up for you.

Do you work with companies outside Ecuador?

Yes. The systems account for time zone and currency per company, and we regularly work remotely. Meetings are scheduled around your time zone.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, doesn't show ads, and doesn't build profiles of the people who visit it. That's why you won't see an "Accept all" banner — there's nothing to consent to.

The only thing saved in your browser

If you switch between light and dark theme, that preference is saved in your own browser so it's remembered next time. It never leaves your device and doesn't identify anyone. You can clear it by clearing the site's data.

Visit measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which according to its documentation doesn't use cookies, browser storage, or visitor fingerprinting. It only gives us aggregate data: how many visits there were, from which country, and which sections are viewed most. It can't tell who you are or track you across other sites.

We also show a public visit counter in the footer. It is literally a running total: we don't store your IP address, your browser, or anything that could tell one visit apart from another.

What we don't do

  • We don't install any kind of cookies.
  • We don't show ads or personalized content.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties.
